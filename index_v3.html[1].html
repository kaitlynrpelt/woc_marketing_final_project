About the Process

Who Uses This Process?

Patients use online scheduling to request routine checkups, follow-up visits, general consultations, and other healthcare appointments.

Why Is It Important?

Scheduling helps patients access care and helps healthcare offices organize visits and communicate appointment details.

Current Scheduling Challenges

Patients may not know which provider or appointment type to choose. Traditional websites can make it difficult to see available times, and patients may need to call the office to change a visit.

How We Improved the Process

The provider directory and calendar from Draft 2 are now connected to an appointment-type guide, demo rescheduling and cancellation, and a preparation checklist. Color-coded sections help patients quickly recognize each step of the process.
