**Track G:** Marketing

**Team:** Lily McPherson, Kaitlyn Pelt, Chloe Gay

## WOC Problem Statement
Our track covers digital marketing analytics,
campaign tracking, CRM integration, and customer acquisition. The specific
problem we will focus on is how can the WOC use real digital marketing data to
find its true cost of acquiring customers? We chose this track over the others
because we are interested in learning more about the world of marketing and data
analytics. Our preliminary recommendation is, “the WOC should interpret
marketing data in order to calculate its actual customer acquisition cost.”

## How the Artifacts Build Toward the Final Recommendation

Together, these five labs trace the path from tools to a rollout plan for calculating the WOC's true customer acquisition cost. Lab 2 set up the GitHub repository that keeps our work organized and documented. Lab 3's Thunkable mobile app puts channel-level spend, clicks, conversions, revenue, and customer acquisition cost in one simple view, which is the foundation for the cost calculation. Lab 7 shows how the WOC can quickly build its own web presence with ChatGPT, creating entry points where campaign traffic and conversions can be tracked. Lab 8's AI social media assistant, built for the Utah Jazz, shows how a marketing team can start from audience data, set a measurable goal, and produce consistent content at low cost, with human review of every post. Lab 10 then decides what the WOC should handle in-house versus outsource, which shapes who owns the analytics and how much it costs to run. The rollout plan follows that order: track the data, view it by channel, use low-cost AI-assisted content to drive campaign activity, and assign ownership based on the Lab 10 decision, so the WOC can interpret its marketing data and know what it actually costs to acquire a customer.
